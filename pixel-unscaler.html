Instructions

• Cell Click: Click a cell to select its color in palette.
• Shift + Click: Add cell's color to active palette selection.
• Alt + Click: Set cell's color as the target merge color.
• Drag Lines: Hover over cyan grid cuts to snap them to integer pixels.
• Combine Colors: Check colors in the Palette, select the target, and click Combine.
• Highlight: Hover over any palette color to highlight matching cells on the canvas.
• Pan & Zoom: Scroll to zoom. Hold Space or Middle Click to pan.
• Add / Remove Cuts: Double-Click in a cell to split it. Right-Click a line to delete.
